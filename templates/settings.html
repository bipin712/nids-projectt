📊 Rule Thresholds (Read-Only)

Denial of Service (DoS) 100 pkts / sec
Triggered if packets from a single source exceed 100 in 1 second.
Port Scan 20 ports / 10 sec
Triggered if unique destinations ports from a source exceed 20 in 10 seconds.
SYN Flood 200 SYN / 10 sec
Triggered if TCP SYN packets from a source exceed 200 in 10 seconds.
ICMP Flood 50 ICMP / 5 sec
Triggered if ICMP ping packets from a source exceed 50 in 5 seconds.
Brute Force 5 RST / 30 sec
Triggered if TCP RST packets from a source exceed 5 in 30 seconds.

Note: Thresholds are configured in detector.py
